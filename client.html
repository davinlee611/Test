How is the BHS calculated?

Confirmed Basic Healthcare Sum amounts are used for cohorts turning 65 up to 2026.

From 2027, the Basic Healthcare Sum is projected to increase by 4.72% each year and is rounded to the nearest $500.

Before age 65, the applicable limit changes annually. The Basic Healthcare Sum in the year the client turns 65 is fixed for life.

When MA reaches the applicable limit, excess allocation is redirected to SA before age 55. From age 55, excess is redirected to RA until the client’s projected cohort FRS is reached, after which it is redirected to OA.
